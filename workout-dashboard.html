DIY Carb Gel 2:1 malto : fructose
Jeukendrup classic · ~30 g carbs per gel
Batch 10 gels
Maltodextrin (DE 10–19) 200 g
Fructose (crystalline) 100 g
Warm water (~60 °C) 115 ml
Citric acid 2.5 g
Per gel: ~30 g carbs
Method
  1. Warm the water to roughly 60 °C — hot tap water works.
  2. Whisk in citric acid until fully dissolved.
  3. Dissolve the fructose, then add maltodextrin gradually while whisking.
  4. Stir in any natural flavouring.
  5. Cool, transfer to refillable flasks, refrigerate and use within 5–7 days.
